Last Updated: May 2, 2026

Welcome to HappyBobux ("we", "our", or "us"). We are committed to protecting your personal information and your right to privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and share information when you use our mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information that you provide directly to us, as well as information collected automatically through the app:

  • Personal Data: Email address (used for account authentication) and Roblox Username (provided by you for reward processing).

We use the collected data for the following purposes:

  • To analyze app performance and improve user experience.

3. Third-Party Services

Our app uses third-party services that may collect information used to identify you:

4. GDPR Compliance (User Rights)

If you are a resident of the European Economic Area (EEA), you have certain data protection rights under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR):

  • The right to access, update, or delete the information we have on you.
  • The right of rectification.
  • The right to object or restrict processing.
  • The right to data portability.

5. CalOPPA & California Privacy Rights

In accordance with the California Online Privacy Protection Act (CalOPPA), we agree to the following:

  • Users can visit our site anonymously (via our policy link).
  • Users will be notified of any privacy policy changes on our Privacy Policy Page.

6. Children's Privacy

Our Service does not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and you are aware that your child has provided us with personal data, please contact us.

We will retain your personal information only for as long as is necessary for the purposes set out in this Privacy Policy, such as processing pending withdrawals or maintaining your account history.
