Privacy Policy

This privacy policy is applicable to the Happy Bobux app (hereinafter referred to as "Application") for mobile devices, which was developed by JurekDev (hereinafter referred to as "Service Provider") as an Ad Supported service. This service is provided "AS IS".

What information does the Application obtain and how is it used?

The Application acquires the information you supply when you download and register the Application. Registration with the Service Provider is optional but may be required to access certain features like redeeming rewards.

When you register and use the Application, you generally provide:

  • Your email address (used for login and account synchronization).
  • Transaction information, such as when you earn or redeem Happy Coins.
  • Roblox username (only when requesting a withdrawal).

Push Notifications

The Application uses Firebase Messaging to send push notifications to your device. These notifications may include information about "Happy Half-Hour" events, "Happy Boost" activations by administrators, and status updates regarding your withdrawal requests. You can opt-out of receiving these notifications at any time through your device's settings.

Automatically Collected Information

In addition, the Application may collect certain information automatically, including, but not limited to, the type of mobile device you use, your mobile devices unique device ID, the IP address of your mobile device, your mobile operating system, and information about the way you use the Application.

Third Party Access

Only aggregated, anonymized data is periodically transmitted to external services to help the Service Provider improve the Application and service. The Service Provider may share your information with third parties only in the ways that are described in this privacy statement.

The Application utilizes third-party services that have their own Privacy Policies:

  • Firebase Cloud Messaging (Push Notifications)
